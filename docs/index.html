Protein Tracker

One number on your wrist: how many grams you have left today.

Just protein

No calorie counting. No AI photo guessing. No four-thousand-row food database to scroll before you can log a chicken breast. Set a daily gram target, see what is left, and add what you eat in one tap.

It counts what you already log

Protein Tracker reads dietary protein from Apple Health, so grams logged in another food app count here automatically. The Sources screen names every app writing protein today, what each contributed, and how long ago, so you always know whether the number in front of you is current. If two apps are logging the same meals, turn one off.

Grams you add here are written back to Apple Health as real dietary protein, so everything else you use sees them too.

The watch is the point

Three buttons set to the amounts you eat over and over, plus a Digital Crown dial for everything else. Your usual shake takes about three seconds without touching your phone. Complications keep grams-left on your watch face; widgets do the same on iPhone.

Who it is for

Lifters and strength trainees

Working to a gram target, and tired of opening a calorie diary to log a shake.

People on GLP-1 medication

Appetite is down, so protein has to be deliberate.

Post-bariatric patients

Following the target the clinic set, without a full food diary.

Private by default

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Your protein data lives in Apple Health and on your devices, and nowhere else.
