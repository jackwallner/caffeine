Accuracy

Totals are only as accurate as the data behind them: what you enter, and what other apps write to Apple Health. Apple Health, WidgetKit, and watchOS control when background updates and complication refreshes happen, so a displayed value can lag behind reality. The app shows you when each source last wrote so you can judge for yourself. Do not rely on it where an error would matter clinically.
