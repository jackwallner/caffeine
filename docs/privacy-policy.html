Privacy Policy

Caffeine Tracker is designed to keep caffeine records on your devices and in Apple Health. We do not operate an account system, sell personal information, or use advertising trackers.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads and writes dietary caffeine in Apple Health. This is used to show intake, current estimates, bedtime forecasts, history, sources, widgets, and Watch complications. Health data is processed on your devices. It is not sent to our servers, RevenueCat, or advertisers.

Data stored on device

The app stores settings, cached forecast values, excluded Health sources, and local fallback entries on your devices and in the app's shared container. Apple may synchronize eligible device and Health data according to your iCloud and Health settings.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, product identifiers, entitlement status, and purchase events so the app can provide Caffeine+ access. RevenueCat does not receive caffeine or Health data.

Notifications

If enabled, bedtime reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification content is generated from the local forecast.

Data deletion

You can delete caffeine entries in Apple Health, remove local app data by deleting the app, and revoke Health access in Settings. Purchase records remain subject to Apple's policies.
