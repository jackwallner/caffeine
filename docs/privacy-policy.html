Privacy Policy

Protein Tracker - Grams Left. Last updated 4 August 2026.

The short version

Protein Tracker has no account system, no server, and no analytics. It never sends your health data anywhere. Everything the app knows about you stays on your devices and in Apple Health.

What the app stores, and where

Your daily protein target, your quick-add presets, which sources you have switched off, and your display preferences are stored on your device in a private app group shared between the iPhone app, the Apple Watch app, and the widgets. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Protein entries you add are written to Apple Health as dietary protein samples, under your control and subject to the permission you grant. If you decline permission for Protein Tracker to write to Apple Health, entries are stored on your device instead and are moved into Apple Health later only if you allow it.

The app keeps a small cache of your daily protein total on the device so the widgets and the watch complication can display it without querying Apple Health, which extensions are not permitted to do.

Apple Health

With your permission, Protein Tracker reads dietary protein samples from Apple Health, including which app wrote each one, so it can show you what is counting toward your day. If you ask it to suggest a target, it reads your most recent body weight. Health data is read on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or used for advertising. You can review or revoke this access at any time in the Health app under your profile, Privacy, Apps.

What is not collected

No name, email address, or account. No advertising identifier. No usage analytics or crash reporting SDK. No third-party trackers. No location.

Purchases

Protein+ purchases are processed by Apple. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous, app-generated identifier and the purchase receipt so the app can tell whether Protein+ is active. RevenueCat does not receive your protein data, your health data, or any information that identifies you. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device by the app. Nothing is sent through a push server.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device, including its settings and its cached totals. Protein samples the app wrote to Apple Health belong to Apple Health, and can be deleted there under Browse, Nutrition, Protein.

Children

Protein Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
