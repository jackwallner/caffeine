Caffeine Tracker turns a caffeine log into a bedtime forecast.

Log caffeine in milligrams and see three different answers: how much you consumed today, how much may remain now, and how much may remain at your usual bedtime. Before you add another coffee, tea, pre-workout, or energy drink, preview the dose and time first.

PREVIEW BEFORE YOU LOG

Choose any dose and time. Caffeine shows the estimated change to your bedtime forecast before anything is saved. The Planner also calculates the latest modeled time that dose fits under your personal bedtime preference.

A preference is not a safety limit. It is simply a number you choose for your own forecast.

A MODEL THAT SHOWS ITS ASSUMPTIONS

The default estimate uses a five-hour half-life. You can adjust it, and the main forecast also shows a reference range based on four to six hours. Every value is described as an estimate, never as caffeine measured in your blood or body.

ONE SOURCE-AWARE TIMELINE

Caffeine reads dietary caffeine from Apple Health and writes the milligrams you add back to Health. The Sources screen shows which apps contributed and lets you exclude an external source without losing your own entries.

ON IPHONE AND APPLE WATCH

Preview and log from your phone or wrist. Widgets and complications keep the estimated amount remaining visible while time passes.

FREE CORE

Logging, drink previews, the current estimate, the bedtime forecast, source controls, Apple Watch logging, widgets, complications, and seven days of history are free.

Caffeine+ adds full history and trends, custom quick-preview amounts, and a bedtime estimate reminder.

IMPORTANT

Caffeine uses a mathematical half-life model for personal awareness. Estimates vary by person, dose, medication, pregnancy, smoking status, and other factors. The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, prescribe, or provide medical advice.
