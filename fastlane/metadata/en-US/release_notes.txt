Meet Caffeine, which finds your cutoff in your own sleep record.

- Read the sleep already in Apple Health and find the bedtime estimate above which your own nights ran measurably shorter
- Wait for twenty-one nights before answering, and say "no clear difference" when that is the answer
- Preview any dose and time before logging it, against your current and proposed bedtime estimates
- Estimate what may remain now and at bedtime, with a visible four to six hour half-life range
- Read and write dietary caffeine in Apple Health, with per-source controls
- Log from iPhone or Apple Watch, with widgets and complications
