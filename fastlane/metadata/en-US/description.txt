One number, on your wrist: how many grams of protein you have left today.

Protein Tracker does one job. It is not a calorie counter, it does not scan your food with AI, and it will never ask you to search a database of four thousand foods to log a chicken breast. You set a daily gram target. You see what is left. You add what you eat in one tap.

WHERE THE NUMBER COMES FROM

Protein Tracker reads dietary protein from Apple Health, so grams you already log in another food app count here automatically. The Sources screen shows every app writing protein today, what each one contributed, and how long ago, so you always know whether the number in front of you is current. If two food apps are both logging the same meals, turn one off.

Grams you add here are written back to Apple Health as real dietary protein, so every other app you use sees them too.

ON YOUR WRIST

The Apple Watch app is the point. Three buttons, set to the amounts you eat over and over, and a Digital Crown dial for everything else. Adding your usual shake takes about three seconds without touching your phone. Complications for the circular, rectangular, inline, and corner families keep grams-left on your watch face, and iPhone widgets do the same on your home and lock screen.

WHO IT IS FOR

- Lifters and strength trainees working to a gram target
- People on GLP-1 medication who need protein to be deliberate while appetite is down
- Post-bariatric patients following the target their clinic set
- Anyone who was told a number and wants to hit it without a food diary

Setup asks which of these you are, then helps you enter your target. Medical audiences enter the number they already use or were given. One app, one number.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Your protein data lives in Apple Health and on your devices, and nowhere else.

FREE AND PROTEIN+
