## Metadata

- Name: `Caffeine Tracker: Bedtime`
- Subtitle: `Preview What's Left Tonight`
- Keywords: `coffee,intake,calculator,sleep,cutoff,half-life,energy,drink,log,timer,metabolism,widget,watch,mg`
- Category: Health & Fitness

The name carries the highest-interest exact phrase. `Bedtime` establishes the
use case without claiming sleep treatment. The subtitle describes the preview
interaction that differentiates the app from a basic caffeine log.

## Product guardrail

The product is not a renamed total counter. The required funnel is:

```text
choose dose and time
        ↓
preview current versus proposed bedtime estimate
        ↓
see dose-specific latest modeled time
        ↓
decide whether to log
```

Screenshots and review notes must demonstrate this sequence. Logging alone is
not sufficient to communicate the app's concept.

## Screenshot messages

Use one period-free header with no prices, discounts, or `free` claims:

1. Know what may remain at bedtime
2. Preview a drink before you log it
3. Your latest time changes with the dose
4. See the pattern beyond today
5. Check the forecast from your wrist

## Claim language

Use `estimate`, `modeled`, `may remain`, and `bedtime preference`. Never call a
forecast a blood or body measurement, a safe limit, a diagnosis, or a guarantee
that a user will sleep.
